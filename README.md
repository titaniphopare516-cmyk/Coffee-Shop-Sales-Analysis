# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes coffee shop sales data using Power BI to understand sales performance, order trends, product performance, store locations, and customer purchasing patterns.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of key business KPIs and helps identify high-performing products, locations, days, and hours.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance
- Analyze total orders and quantity sold
- Identify top-performing product categories
- Identify top-selling products
- Compare sales across store locations
- Analyze weekday vs weekend sales
- Analyze sales by day and hour
- Compare monthly sales performance
- Identify sales trends and business opportunities

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### Sales Trend

The dashboard shows sales trends over the selected period and helps identify changes in daily sales performance.

### Sales by Product Category

The dashboard compares sales across different product categories such as Coffee, Tea, Bakery, Drinking Chocolate, and Coffee Beans.

### Top 10 Products

The dashboard highlights the top-selling products based on sales, including Barista Espresso, Brewed Chai Tea, Hot Chocolate, and other products.

### Sales by Store Location

Sales performance is analyzed across different store locations, including Hell's Kitchen, Astoria, and Lower Manhattan.

### Weekday vs Weekend

The dashboard compares sales performance between weekdays and weekends.

### Sales by Day and Hour

The dashboard provides day-wise and hourly sales analysis to identify high-performing periods.
